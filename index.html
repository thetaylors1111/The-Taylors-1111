Our Story

Reconnected through space and time.

Katina grew up in Bogue Chitto, Mississippi, and Michael grew up just down the road in Summit — two small towns, two different high schools. It took a party to finally put them in the same room, and somewhere between introductions and a T-Pain song, sparks flew. "Buy U A Drank" became ours before we even knew what it meant. It felt exactly like it should: like being reconnected across space and time.

Life took us in different directions for a season, but the connection never completely disappeared. Years later, when our paths crossed again, it felt less like a new beginning and more like picking up where we had left off.
